# Which Vattel did the colonists cite? Sources of the passages, 1762–1776

*Data report, built by `tools/reports/vattel_american.py` from the edition vattel-1773 1.19 (its reuse tables and divergence notes) and from a list of American citations of Vattel compiled from three studies: W. Ossipow and D. Gerber, "The Reception of Vattel's Law of Nations in the American Colonies", *American Journal of Legal History* 57 (2017); M. Somos, "Vattel's Reception in British America, 1761–1775", in P. Schröder (ed.), *Concepts and Contexts of Vattel's Political and Legal Thought* (2021); J. S. Schmeeckle, "Vattel's *The Law of Nations* and the Declaration of Independence" (2006/2016). The list is [data/vattel-american-citations.json](data/vattel-american-citations.json).*

The three studies report 81 citations of Vattel by American writers between 1762 and 1776. 62 of them name or quote a passage; together they concern 42 §§. The studies also propose 15 further §§ as sources of documents of 1775–1777 that do not cite Vattel.

## The cited §§ and their sources

Each § is classed by its strongest match in the edition's tables. *Wolff, close*: a close paraphrase, translation or verbatim match in Wolff's *Jus gentium*, *Institutiones* or *Jus naturae* VIII. *Other source*: no close match in Wolff, but a close match in another source, or a loose one where Wolff has none (Grotius, Pufendorf, Barbeyrac's notes, Burlamaqui, Formey, Vattel's earlier works). *Wolff, loose*: a loose paraphrase of Wolff and no close match elsewhere. *No source found*: none of these. *Departs from Wolff*: a divergence note of the edition, where Vattel had Wolff's passage before him and reached the opposite point.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

24 of the 42 cited §§ have a close match in Wolff. If the cited §§ were drawn from their books at random, about 25 would. 4 cited §§ depart from a passage of Wolff: [Prél. 21](../viewer/?ed=vattel-1773#Prelim.21), [I.220](../viewer/?ed=vattel-1773#I.220), [I.223](../viewer/?ed=vattel-1773#I.223), [III.201](../viewer/?ed=vattel-1773#III.201).

## Notes

- The tables are the edition's own (vattel-1773 1.19), found by retrieval and judged by a language model, and partly read a second time. A § without a match may still have a source the collection does not hold. The collection has no table for Locke, Montesquieu's other works or the British authors the colonists also read.
- "Cited by" names the writers of the documents, with Ossipow and Gerber's, Somos's and Schmeeckle's attributions. Several writers cited several §§ in one document; each § counts once per document in the citation counts.
- The list follows the three studies. It is not a census of American citations of Vattel.
