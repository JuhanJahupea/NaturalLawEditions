## Method and limits

- `tools/transcription/crousaz_ocr.py`: Google's OCR text layer of the PDF, page by page. The running head is kept apart as `head`. Nothing is corrected by hand.
- The OCR keeps long s as `ſ` on most pages and reads it as f on others ("fon", "fans"). Words are sometimes run together. The reuse search folds s and f.
- Check against the *Défense*: of the 73 quotations Vattel gives with a page of the *Examen*, 64 share wording with that page, 6 with the next page, 3 with neither. One of the three is a paraphrase on p. 94 ("douze œufs"), cited as pp. 93–94.
